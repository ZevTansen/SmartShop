About Us

SmartShop is a client-side storefront: products load live from a public API, the cart, coupons and balance update in real time, and your balance persists across reloads. We kept the design minimal so the focus stays on the products, building it entirely with HTML, Tailwind CSS and plain JavaScript. No frameworks, no page reloads, and it works smoothly on both mobile and desktop.
